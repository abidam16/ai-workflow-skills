# ADR Quality Bar

A good ADR must be:

## Bounded

One ADR records one decision only.

## Evidence-based

It names the source artifacts, constraints, and observations that created the need for the decision.

## Alternative-aware

It includes credible alternatives and explains why they were rejected.

## Trade-off honest

It records costs, risks, limitations, and future consequences, not only benefits.

## Architecture-linked

It states whether `ARCHITECTURE.md` or an initiative architecture document must be updated or linked.

## Implementation-useful

Future plans and implementations should understand the constraints created by the decision.

## Review-useful

Reviewers should be able to check whether code obeys the ADR.

## Concise

The ADR should not become a broad design document. Move broad system shape to architecture and task steps to plan.

## Complete terminal handoff

Every ADR output must end with `Concrete Next Step` and route to one concrete next action.
