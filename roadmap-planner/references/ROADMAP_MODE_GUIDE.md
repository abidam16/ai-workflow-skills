# Roadmap Mode Guide

## Product Roadmap

Use when the roadmap acts as a durable product-level index across initiatives.

Best for:

- showing active, deferred, completed, and upcoming initiatives
- preserving strategic direction
- linking to initiative roadmaps
- keeping execution detail out of the product-level document

Avoid:

- detailed task breakdown
- long phase-by-phase execution content
- architecture design detail

## Initiative Roadmap

Use when one feature, migration, refactor, platform change, or reliability effort needs phased delivery.

Best for:

- sequencing a specific initiative
- preparing plan handoff candidates
- exposing dependencies, risks, and deferrals
- showing architecture-driven phase order

Default to initiative roadmap when the work is meant to become executable plans.
