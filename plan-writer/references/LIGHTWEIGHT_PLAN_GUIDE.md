# LIGHTWEIGHT_PLAN_GUIDE.md

Use a lightweight plan when the task is small, local, low-risk, and does not need PRD, Architecture, ADR, or Roadmap work.

A lightweight plan is still binding. It must be specific enough for implementation and review.

## Required Decisions

- What is the one objective?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- Why is PRD not needed?
- Why is Architecture not needed?
- Why is ADR not needed?
- Why is Roadmap not needed?
- What validates completion?
- What condition escalates out of lightweight mode?

## Escalate Instead Of Planning When

- behavior is ambiguous
- boundaries or source-of-truth are unclear
- implementation needs a durable decision
- staged rollout/sequencing matters
- task scope cannot be expressed as one objective
